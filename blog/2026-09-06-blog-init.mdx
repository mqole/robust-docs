So as of writing, development on the [Space Wizards Development Wiki](https://github.com/space-wizards/docs) has stalled somewhat. There are a number of reasons for this. Most prominent is obviously the recent leadership transfer, but also of significance is the decision to move documentation off mdbook and onto [**Outline**](https://github.com/outline/outline).

I think the decision to move off mdbook is fantastic.

I *cannot stand* Outline.

{/* truncate */}

Previously, I had attempted to migrate all the docs pages onto Outline, and I very quickly got sick of having to deal with the weird syntax and dynamic page loading. Outline is probably fantastic for collaborative notetaking (which would be helpful for design docs...!) but it's kind of miserable to attempt writing engine documentation on that thing.

So, here's my own documentation site ~~with blackjack and hookers~~. Most of the pages here will not be my own writing, instead taken from the upstream docs repo (previously linked) and shared under the [MPL 2.0](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/). I'm using [**Docusaurus**](https://docusaurus.io/) for this site, after finding success with it writing documentation for Macrocosm previously.

Of note, this site **will not** include information from the upstream docs that doesn't pertain specifically to development. That means none of the following:
- Design docs & feature proposals
- Maintainer meeting minutes
- Administration meeting minutes
- Community policy
- Design principles (*maybe? undecided on this one.)*
- Staff information (*also undecided.*)

Ultimately I just want this to be a nice and handy reference guide anyone who wants to get into SS14 development can use at any skill level. Of course, in a perfect world most of the engine and game documentation would be in the code itself. But this companion site will hopefully lessen that barrier to entry.

At the moment I have no plans to support languages other than English on this site. I'm open to it, but I'm 1. monolingual and 2. more interested in getting all the requisite pages up before looking into adding more features to this site.

If you want to contribute, feel free to make a pull request, chuck me an email, DM me on Discord, whatever. I'm flying by the seat of my pants just a bit here but we'll see what happens.
